PROJECT OVERVIEW

Overview

ResearchMind is a next-generation AI Research Assistant built on a Multi-Agent Architecture. Instead of relying on a single AI model, the platform uses multiple specialized AI agents that collaborate together to automate the complete research lifecycle. From searching reliable information, extracting webpage content, writing professional research reports, and evaluating report quality, every task is handled autonomously, providing users with comprehensive, well-structured, and downloadable research reports.

01

Problem Statement

Researchers, students, and professionals spend hours searching multiple websites, collecting information, reading articles, and manually preparing research reports. Traditional search engines only provide links rather than complete research, making the overall process slow, repetitive, and time-consuming.

The challenge was to develop an intelligent platform capable of automating the entire research workflow using autonomous AI agents that collaborate together to search, analyze, generate, and evaluate research content.

02

Solution Overview

ResearchMind is an Autonomous Multi-Agent AI Research Platform where multiple specialized AI agents work together to automate the complete research lifecycle. Each AI agent is responsible for a specific task, enabling intelligent collaboration that improves research quality while significantly reducing manual effort.

Search Agent

Searches the web using Tavily Search API and collects reliable information related to the research topic.

Reader Agent

Extracts detailed webpage content using BeautifulSoup and Requests for further AI processing.

Writer Agent

Generates structured and professional research reports using Mistral AI and LangChain.

Critic Agent

Reviews report quality, identifies improvements, and enhances the final research output.

03

Multi-Agent Workflow

Step 1

User enters a research topic.

Step 2

Search Agent gathers recent information using Tavily Search API.

Step 3

Reader Agent extracts useful webpage content using BeautifulSoup.

Step 4

Writer Agent generates a structured AI research report.

Step 5

Critic Agent reviews the report quality and provides improvements.

Step 6

User downloads the final research report in Markdown format.
