PROJECT OVERVIEW

Overview

The Multimodal AI Research Assistant is an intelligent research platform designed to simplify information retrieval from multiple data sources. Users can upload PDF documents, audio recordings, or provide YouTube URLs. The system converts every source into structured, searchable knowledge using AI-powered pipelines, allowing users to interact through an intelligent chat interface backed by Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

01

Problem Statement

Researchers, students, and professionals often work with multiple information sources such as PDF documents, meeting recordings, and educational YouTube videos. Extracting useful insights from these resources manually is time-consuming and inefficient.

Traditional search methods only perform keyword matching and fail to understand context or relationships between different pieces of information.

There was a need for an AI-powered assistant capable of understanding documents, audio, and videos while providing intelligent, context-aware answers through natural conversation.

02

Solution Overview

The solution is a Multimodal AI Research Assistant capable of processing multiple content formats and converting them into a unified searchable knowledge base.

PDF Intelligence

Extracts text, creates embeddings, summarizes documents, and enables contextual question answering.

Audio Transcription

Converts speech into text using Whisper AI and generates summaries with action items.

YouTube Processing

Automatically extracts transcripts, summarizes content, and allows semantic search.

AI Chat

Provides context-aware conversations using Retrieval-Augmented Generation and LLMs.
