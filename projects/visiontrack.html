PROJECT OVERVIEW

Overview

VisionTrack is an AI-powered attendance management platform developed to eliminate traditional manual attendance systems through intelligent face recognition. The platform enables employees to securely register their facial data, authenticate themselves using real-time webcam recognition, and automatically record attendance. Administrators can monitor attendance, analyze employee performance, track absenteeism, and manage employee records through interactive dashboards and visual analytics. By combining Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and modern web technologies, VisionTrack provides a secure, accurate, and efficient attendance solution for organizations.

01

Problem Statement

Traditional attendance systems rely on manual registers, RFID cards, or biometric devices that are often time-consuming, prone to proxy attendance, and require continuous administrative monitoring.

Organizations require a secure, contactless, and automated attendance solution capable of accurately identifying employees while providing real-time attendance records, analytics, and centralized employee management.

02

Solution Overview

VisionTrack is an AI-powered attendance platform that combines Computer Vision and Machine Learning to automate employee attendance using real-time face recognition. Employees securely register their facial data, authenticate through webcam recognition, and instantly mark attendance. Administrators gain access to attendance reports, employee statistics, absentee monitoring, and visual analytics through a modern dashboard.

Employee Registration

Secure face registration with employee profile creation.

Face Recognition

Interactive dashboards showing attendance history and employee insights.

Absentee Alerts

Automatically warns administrators when monthly absences exceed limits.

03

Recognition Workflow

Step 1

Employee logs into VisionTrack.

Step 2

Webcam captures employee's face.

Step 3

OpenCV detects the face from the live video frame.

Step 4

KNN compares facial embeddings with registered employees.

Step 5

Attendance is recorded automatically.

Step 6

Dashboard and analytics update instantly.

Challenges & Learnings

Major Challenges

  • Achieving accurate face recognition under different lighting conditions.
  • Synchronizing employee information with facial embeddings.
  • Reducing false face matches during real-time recognition.
  • Preventing duplicate attendance records.
  • Designing responsive admin and employee dashboards.

Key Learnings

  • Computer Vision with OpenCV.
  • Machine Learning using K-Nearest Neighbors.
  • Real-time webcam processing.
  • Face Recognition pipeline development.
  • Interactive dashboard development with Streamlit.
  • Data visualization and attendance analytics.
